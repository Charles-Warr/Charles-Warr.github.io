Frequently asked questions

Want to know more or have a question? You can always reach us at support@munnuo.com but here are some of our most frequent questions.

How do I report a bug in the game?

You can report bugs through our Support Form or by emailing us at support@munnuo.com with details and screenshots.

Where can I find game updates and patch notes?

Check out our News section for the latest updates, or follow us on social media for real-time announcements.

How do I request a refund?

Refund requests are managed through the platform you purchased the game on (Steam, App Store, etc.).

How can I contact customer support directly?

Our Support Team is available via the Contact Form or at support@munnuo.com for assistance with any issues.
