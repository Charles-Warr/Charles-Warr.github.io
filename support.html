What to include in your ticket

Providing details up front helps us resolve your issue faster.

  1. Game title and platform (PC, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, mobile).
  2. Your region and language.
  3. A clear description of the issue and when it started.
  4. Any error messages or codes you see.
  5. Steps you have already tried (reinstall, restart, etc.).

For technical issues, you can also attach screenshots or logs if available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from players and customers.

How do I recover my account?

Use the “Forgot password” option on the login screen. If you no longer have access to your email, contact support with proof of ownership (purchase receipts, usernames, etc.).

What platforms do you support?

Platform availability varies by title, but we generally support PC, major consoles, and select mobile devices. Check each game’s store page for details.

How do I report a bug?

Use the in‑game report tool where available, or submit a ticket with steps to reproduce, screenshots, and your platform information.

Can I request a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on the platform and time since purchase. Start with the store where you bought the game, then contact us if you need more help.

Are your servers down?

Check our official server status page or social channels for real‑time updates on outages, maintenance, and known issues.

How do I appeal a ban?

Submit a ticket under “Account & Enforcement” with your account ID and any relevant details. Appeals are reviewed manually and may take several days.
